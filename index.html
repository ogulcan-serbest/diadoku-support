Helpful information

Diadoku does not require an account. Your level progress, daily streak, settings, hints, and best results are stored locally on your device.

If the app is not behaving as expected, first close and reopen it and make sure the latest version of iOS is installed. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description when contacting support.

You can also submit and vote on feature requests from Settings → Feedback inside Diadoku.
