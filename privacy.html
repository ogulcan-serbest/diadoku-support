Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Diadoku, developed by Ogulcan Serbest ("Diadoku", "we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the Diadoku iOS app.

Information stored on your device and in iCloud

Diadoku does not require an account. Game progress, completed levels, best results, stars, daily streak, hint balance, profile gem and frame, tutorial state, and audio, haptic, appearance and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage.

If iCloud is turned on for Diadoku on your device, Apple's iCloud key-value storage keeps a copy of your game progress and of your star league player identity (see below), so another device signed in to the same Apple Account can continue where you left off. This copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple's terms; we cannot access your iCloud account. You can turn this off for Diadoku in iOS Settings under your Apple Account and iCloud.

Star league

Diadoku has a weekly star league and an all-time leaderboard. To take part, the app creates a random player identifier and a random secret on your device; no account, email or Apple ID is used. When you open the app and when you solve puzzles, the app sends the following to our league server:

  • the random player identifier, and the secret, of which the server stores only a one-way hash;
  • your player name (a random name such as "Gem1234" until you change it in your profile), your chosen gem and frame;
  • your total stars and the stars you earned in each league week, and when they last changed;
  • how many levels you have solved, so the app can show you what share of players you are ahead of. This number is not shown to other players;
  • the last day you opened the app, which we use only to count how many players play each day; we keep no history of the days you played;
  • names you report, if you use this option. A name you report is no longer shown to you.

Your player name, gem, frame, stars, rank and when you last earned stars are visible to other players in the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your player name. Names are checked against a list of offensive words, and a name that several players report is hidden until it is changed.

The league server is run on Supabase, hosted in the European Union (Ireland). Like any server, it may briefly log technical connection data such as IP addresses for operation and security. We use league data only to run the leaderboard and the weekly rewards, and to count how many players play each day.

Duels

In a duel, two players solve the same board. When you play a duel, the app sends the following to our league server: the board you got, when and where you placed each diamond (marks are not sent), and the result. The server uses this to check the result and to count your trophies and your duels of the day. Your run may later be played back to another player as their opponent, together with your player name, gem, frame and trophies; they only see how many diamonds you had placed and how many hearts you had left, never where. Your trophies appear in the trophy ranking. Duel data is kept on the same server in the European Union as the league, and we use it only to run the duels and the trophy ranking.

Challenges by link

After a won duel or a solved daily puzzle you can challenge a friend with a link (diadoku.com/d/ followed by a short code). When you create a challenge, the app sends our league server the board and your run (when and where you placed each diamond; marks are not sent). Anyone who has the link or the code can see your player name, gem, frame, the board size and your time, also on the web page diadoku.com/d/CODE, and can play the same board against your run; they see how many diamonds you had placed and how many hearts you had left, never where. When someone plays your challenge, the server keeps their run and result the same way, and tells you their player name and result, as a notification if you allow notifications. Challenges give no trophies and do not appear in any ranking. They are kept for 30 days, on the same server in the European Union as the league.

Friends and friendly duels

You can add other players as friends and play live duels against them. Other players can find you by your player name. For this, the app and our league server use:

  • your friends and friend requests: who asked whom, and when a request was accepted;
  • when you last had the app open, so your friends can see whether you are online or when you were last seen;
  • invitations to a friendly duel and whether they were accepted, and for each friendly duel the board, when and where you placed each diamond (marks are not sent) and the result. During the duel your friend sees how many diamonds you have placed and how many hearts you have left, and both of you see how many friendly duels each of you has won against the other;
  • players you block, who can then no longer find you or send you requests, and names you report.

Friendly duels give no trophies and do not appear in any ranking. The live connection during a duel runs through the same server in the European Union.

Notifications from friends. If you allow notifications, the app sends your iPhone's push token (an address Apple gives your device for this app) to our league server. We use it only to tell you, while the app is closed, that a friend invites you to a duel, sent you a friend request or played your challenge. These notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service and contain your friend's player name. When you turn off notifications for Diadoku, the app removes the token from our server, and a token that Apple reports as no longer valid is deleted too.

Usage analytics

To understand how the game is played and where it can be improved, Diadoku uses PostHog, hosted in the European Union. The app sends anonymous events such as levels started, completed, failed or left, hints and optional ads used, tutorial steps, which home screen buttons and settings are used, results shared, daily streaks, and when the app is opened. Each event comes with technical information such as the device model, iOS version, app version, language and time zone, and an anonymous random identifier for this installation.

Analytics never include your name, email, advertising identifier, league player name or screen recordings, and they are not used for advertising or to track you across apps. PostHog may use your IP address to estimate an approximate location (such as the country), and we have set PostHog not to store the IP address itself. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics are only sent if you agree in the consent form to storing information on your device and to measuring content performance. We keep analytics data for up to one year. See PostHog's Privacy Policy for more information.

Advertising and third-party services

Diadoku uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • device or advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • advertising data and interactions with ads;
  • app interactions, diagnostics, crash information, and performance information.

This information may be used to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertisements and related services. Google processes information under its own terms and policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

Feature requests and feedback

Diadoku uses WishKit to provide the in-app feedback board and the chat for writing to us. When you open or use them, WishKit may process:

  • an anonymous, randomly generated identifier for this app installation;
  • product interactions such as votes and feedback activity; and
  • the feature requests and messages you choose to submit.

This information is used to operate the feedback board and the chat, to associate submissions, votes and messages with an installation, and to let us read and answer your messages. Diadoku does not send your email address, name, payment information, or a Diadoku account identifier to WishKit. Do not include sensitive personal information in feedback or messages you submit. See WishKit's Privacy Policy for more information.

Daily reminders

If you turn on reminders, Diadoku schedules local notifications on your device. They are not sent through a server, and you can turn them off in the app's settings or in iOS Settings at any time. Notifications about friends are described under "Friends and friendly duels".

Consent and tracking choices

Where required, Diadoku asks for consent for advertising and analytics through Google's consent platform. On supported Apple devices, the app may also ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before data is used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. You may decline these requests. Core puzzle gameplay remains available if you decline.

You can review or change relevant choices in iOS Settings. Depending on your region and the choices available in the app, you may also be able to revisit advertising privacy options.

Optional rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are optional. They are requested only when you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game hint. Declining an ad does not prevent normal gameplay.

Children

Diadoku is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising services may apply their own age-related rules and safeguards. Players should not use their real name as their league player name.

Legal bases and your rights

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process data for the star league to provide the leaderboard you take part in (legitimate interest), and we use analytics and personalised advertising only with your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time through the privacy options in the app or in iOS Settings; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal.

You have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Data retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until it is removed by iOS or the app is deleted. The iCloud copy remains in your iCloud account until you remove Diadoku's iCloud data in iOS Settings.

Star league data is kept while the league runs. A friendship or friend request is deleted when you or your friend removes it. To have your league data deleted, email us your current player name, and we will delete your player with its scores, duels, friends and push token. Analytics data is kept for up to one year. Information processed by Google, WishKit or their partners is retained according to their policies.

International processing

Our league server (Supabase) and analytics (PostHog) are hosted in the European Union. Other third-party service providers, such as Google, may process information in countries other than the one where you live, subject to their privacy policies and applicable safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.
