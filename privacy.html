Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Diadoku, developed by Ogulcan Serbest ("Diadoku", "we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the Diadoku iOS app.

Information stored on your device

Diadoku does not require an account. Game progress, completed levels, best results, daily streak, hint balance, tutorial state, and audio or haptic preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. We do not operate a server that receives this game data. Removing the app may remove locally stored game data.

Advertising and third-party services

Diadoku uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • device or advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • advertising data and interactions with ads;
  • app interactions, diagnostics, crash information, and performance information.

This information may be used to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertisements and related services. Google processes information under its own terms and policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

Feature requests and feedback

Diadoku uses WishKit to provide the in-app feedback board. When you open or interact with the board, WishKit may process:

  • an anonymous, randomly generated identifier for this app installation;
  • product interactions such as votes and feedback activity; and
  • the feature requests you choose to submit.

This information is used to operate the feedback board and associate submissions and votes with an installation. Diadoku does not send your email address, name, payment information, or a Diadoku account identifier to WishKit. Do not include sensitive personal information in feedback you submit. See WishKit's Privacy Policy for more information.

Consent and tracking choices

Where required, Diadoku asks for advertising consent through Google's consent platform. On supported Apple devices, the app may also ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before data is used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. You may decline these requests. Core puzzle gameplay remains available if you decline.

You can review or change relevant choices in iOS Settings. Depending on your region and the choices available in the app, you may also be able to revisit advertising privacy options.

Optional rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are optional. They are requested only when you choose to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game hint. Declining an ad does not prevent normal gameplay.

Children

Diadoku is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising services may apply their own age-related rules and safeguards.

Data retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until it is reset in the app, removed by iOS, or the app is deleted. Information processed by Google or its partners is retained according to their policies. You can reset Diadoku progress from the app's settings.

International processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, subject to their privacy policies and applicable safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.
